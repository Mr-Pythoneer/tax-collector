Tax Collector
=============

Needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.


INSTALL

1. Drag "Tax Collector" onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. Open Tax Collector from your Applications folder.
   macOS will say it can't check the app for malware, because it isn't
   from the App Store. Click "Done", then open System Settings > Privacy &
   Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway" next to Tax Collector.
   (It asks for your Mac's password.)

3. Read the welcome screen, then hand the Mac to a parent or friend to
   set the guardian code, and click "Finish Setup".
   Tax Collector then lives in the menu bar (the columns icon) and starts
   every time you log in.

4. Click "Allow" when it asks to send notifications, so you get warnings
   before anything locks.


PROOF CHECKING

Claude checks your proof, using Claude Code. Install it and sign in once,
in Terminal:

    curl -fsSL https://claude.ai/install.sh | bash
    claude auth login

Without it, your guardian has to approve every proof by hand.

No file to show, like a playing test? Leave the files empty and describe
exactly what you did. Vague descriptions get rejected.


GOOD TO KNOW

- During a lock only Arc, Claude, the Microsoft 365 apps, Mail, Zoom,
  VooV Meeting and Activity Monitor stay open. If you use another browser
  (Safari or Chrome), your guardian can allow it in
  Guardian Settings > Extra allowed apps.
- Save your work before deadlines. When a lock starts, other apps are
  asked to quit, then closed after 10 seconds.
- A task is locked in 24 hours before it's due. After that, only your
  guardian can change or delete it.


UNINSTALL

Guardian Settings > Uninstall Tax Collector. It stops the app and moves it
to the Trash. Your tasks stay in ~/Library/Application Support/TaxCollector.
